64yo M with h/o HCV-related cirrhosis and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) measuring 6.2cm on multi-phase CT with contrast; Child Pugh score A (good liver function) but Barcelona stage B (not a candidate for resection or liver transplant).

He was treated with SBRT. He received 45Gy in 5 fractions over 2 weeks. Respiratory gating was used and he was treated on 50% phase (exhale)

 

NOTES on indications for SBRT in HCC:

Barcelona staging provides a treatment algorithm for HCC, which includes Child Pugh score (see calculator).

SBRT reserved for localized HCC that is:

  • unresectable (solitary, no vascular invasion, Childs A, no portal HTN)
  • in a patient not liver transplant candidate (per Milan criteria), and
  • often after other treatments exhausted (RFA, TACE)*

*Evidence for SBRT vs. TACE/RFA: 

  • SBRT is equivalent to TACE, but less toxic, as a bridge to transplant (GI ASCO abstract 2017). 
  • For tumors >2 cm, SBRT is superior to RFA for freedom from local failure (Wahl et al JCO 2016).

Contours (per RTOG 1112)

  • GTV = GTVp + GTVv
    • GTVparenchymal = enhancing lesion on arterial phase imaging. NOTE: Tumor should enhance in arterial phase, then wash out in venous phase (hypointense compared to liver). If no wash out in venous phase, it is likely a blood vessel. Consider reviewing with diagnostic radiologist.
    • GTVvascular = tumor vein thrombus appears hypointense (relative to vessels and liver), esp in venous phase.
  • CTV not used routinely on RTOG 1112.
    • NOTE: Can consider 0-4mm margin for high probability microinvasion (per Wang et al IJROBP 2010) if dose constraints can be met.
  • PTV + 7-10mm for setup and liver motion (goal ≤10mm per RTOG 1112)
    • NOTE: Respiratory gating strongly recommended if margin >2cm.
OARs:
  • Duodenum should be contoured.
  • Bowel should be contoured on levels including PTV or any structure receiving dose >10Gy.

RT Dose (per RTOG 1112): 
  • 27.5-50Gy in 5 fractions, based on normal tissue constraints (see below).
  • Ideally 48hr between treatments though 24-72hr acceptable.
Dose Constraints (per RTOG 1112):
Mean liver dose (MLD) = liver minus all GTVs. MLD takes priority over Veff. If cannot meet liver constraints, reduce Rx by 5Gy.
  • If Rx 50Gy → MLD 13Gy, Veff <25%
  • If Rx 45Gy → MLD 15Gy, Veff 25-29%
  • If Rx 40Gy → MLD 15Gy, Veff 30-34%
  • If Rx 35Gy → MLD 15.5Gy, Veff 35-44%
  • If Rx 30Gy → MLD 17Gy, Veff 45-54%
Other constraints (5-fraction):
  • Esophagus max (to 0.5 cc): 32 Gy
  • Stomach max (to 0.5 cc): 30 Gy
  • Duodenum max (to 0.5 cc): 30 Gy
  • Bowel max (to 0.5 cc): 30 Gy
  • Spinal canal + 5 mm max (0.5cc): 25 Gy
  • Kidneys (bilateral): mean <10Gy 
Anatomy: 
  • Portal Vein forms behind the pancreas, at confluence of SMV and splenic vein. Before reaching liver, divides into left and right (per Radiopaedia).
